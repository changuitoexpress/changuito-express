---
name: YARVIS approvals
description: The user's approval rule for the embedded God Mode assistant.
---

YARVIS, the assistant inside the general administrator's God Mode panel, must confirm before each change.

**Why:** The user selected “Confirmar antes de cada cambio” when asked what permissions YARVIS should have.

**How to apply:** Reading and proposing are separate from execution. Show the concrete change and require an explicit confirmation for each operation, including order assignments, product edits and photos. Do not treat a conversational reply or voice transcript as approval to execute a proposed change.
