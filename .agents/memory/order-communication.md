---
name: Order communication
description: Business purpose of direct customer–courier conversations.
---

Customers and assigned couriers should exchange order messages and product photos directly; the administrator needs to supervise without mediating every exchange.

**Why:** The user said that handling separate customer and courier conversations causes confusion and asked for an internal WhatsApp-like chat to reduce that work.

**How to apply:** For orders placed in the app, keep conversations tied to the relevant order, accessible to its participants and the administrator. Prefer direct communication over redirecting both participants to the administrator. This does not describe the separate WhatsApp CRM; see whatsapp-crm-scope.md.
