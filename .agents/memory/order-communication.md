Customers and assigned couriers should exchange order messages and product photos directly; the administrator needs to supervise without mediating every exchange.

**Why:** The user said that handling separate customer and courier conversations causes confusion and asked for an internal WhatsApp-like chat to reduce that work.

**How to apply:** Keep conversations tied to the relevant order, accessible to its participants and the administrator. Prefer direct communication over redirecting both participants to the administrator.
